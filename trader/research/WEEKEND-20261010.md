## 1. Pre-market exit vs the 09:31 exit (overnight leg)
Question: pre-market marks often looked better than the 09:31 fill (10/9: +1.9 marked, +0.73 realised). Would a pre-market exit (selling a position bought the day before before 09:30 is **not** a day trade) capture more?

| fixed 08:00 pre-market | +0.39% | 1.92 | 60% | +0.46% | 2.92 | 57% |
| fixed 09:00 pre-market | +0.46% | 2.28 | 63% | +0.64% | 2.69 | 53% |

**Verdict: keep the 09:31 exit.** Every pre-market variant has a lower mean. The take-profits hit often (20–23 of 30 at ≤1%), but they cap the large opening gaps that carry the mean. The 10/9 "fade" was a single draw, not a pattern to trade against. No rule change.
