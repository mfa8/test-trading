# Day leg: take-profit and OCO stop — 1-minute re-test (Tue 10/13 pre-market)

**Why.** The broker now offers OCO orders (a take-profit limit plus a stop-market; when one fills the other cancels; regular hours only). That raises the question of whether a stop helps the TZA day leg. Separately, WEEKEND-20261010 §3 said to re-test the TP with 1-minute bars if the live hit rate stayed below 70%. It stands at 4/7.

**Data.**
- TZA 1-minute regular-hours bars for 21 sessions (9/14–10/12): `tza_1m_0914_1012.json`. Script: `tptest1m.py`.
- TZA 5-minute bars for 40 sessions (8/17–10/12): `tza_5m_20261012.json`. Script: `ocotest.py`.

**Simulation assumptions.**
- Exit at the close of the 15:48 bar if neither leg fills.
- The TP fills only when price trades through it by at least 1 cent.
- A stop-market fills 3 cents below the stop.
- On 5-minute bars, if TP and stop are both inside one bar, the stop is assumed to fill first.

## Results: adding an OCO stop

Mean return per session (score in brackets):

| TP | no stop | stop 1% | stop 1.5% | stop 2% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0%, 1-minute bars, 21 sessions | **+0.82% (1.55)** | +0.40% (0.43) | +0.54% (0.56) | +0.76% (1.00) |
| 1.0%, 5-minute bars, 40 sessions | **+0.56%** | +0.37% | +0.48% | +0.53% |

Every stop level, at every TP, lowers both mean and score. TZA's intraday path dips before it pays: about 12 of 21 sessions touch −1% after the entry, and many of those still finish positive.

## Live check (7 legs since TP v1.3)

- Live 09:32 fills sit within −0.26 to +0.11 of the 09:31 bar close. There is no systematic bias.
- **9/28 miss:** high 46.98 against TP 47.00, short by 2 cents.
- **10/9 miss:** high 47.83 against TP 47.90, short by 7 cents.
- **10/12 miss:** high 47.685 against TP 47.86. The simulation also has this one as a miss.
- A TP of 0.75% would have turned 9/28 and 10/9 into hits but given up 0.25% on each of the 4 hits: about +1.8% better over these 7 legs. That is within noise, and the 21-session tests favour 1.0% on mean.

## Decision

- **No change to RULES_v3 §11: TP stays at 1.0%, no intraday stop, no OCO.** Every stop variant tested was worse.
- An OCO with a −2% stop gives up about 0.06% per day of mean (1-minute test). It would only be worth that cost as tail insurance if a wider PAUSE trigger came into play. Not adopted.
- Revisit at the 20-leg mark (about 8 more legs), or if the live hit rate drops below 50%.
