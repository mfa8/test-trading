# Overnight basket: does the 30-night ranking work? (Tue 10/13)

## Trigger
METU kept rising after our 09:31 exit on 10/12 and 10/13. That raised two questions:
1. On days with no day leg (PDT-blocked), should the basket be held through the session instead of sitting in cash?
2. Is METU really the best overnight name?

## Data
Daily regular-hours bars, 2025-10-01 → 2026-10-09: 257 nights, 14-name long universe plus TZA.
- Data files: `daily_1y_20261012.json`, `daily_1y_univ_20261012.json`.
- Scripts: `intraday.py`, `walkfwd.py`.

## 1. Overnight vs intraday by name (250 sessions)

| name | overnight mean/night | intraday mean/day | overnight cumulative | intraday cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NVDL | **+0.44%** (score 0.16, win 57%) | **−0.24%** | +173% | −55% |
| NVDX | +0.40% | −0.24% | +146% | −54% |
| METU | +0.04% (0.01) | −0.01% | −7% | −18% |
| GGLL | +0.09% | +0.23% | +13% | +54% |

**Holding through the session on PDT-blocked days is rejected.**
- NVDA-2x loses intraday (−0.24%/day).
- METU's intraday mean is about zero over 250 days. Its +0.58%/day over the last 30 days is a recent run, not a pattern.
- Cash stays cash on blocked days.

## 2. Walk-forward test of the ranking
Method: re-rank weekly, hold the top 2 distinct underlyings equal-weight, test on the next 137 nights.

| rule | mean/night | sd | score | cumulative |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| top-2 by 20-night score | +0.65% | 4.91 | 0.13 | +106% |
| **top-2 by 30-night score (current §3)** | **+0.39%** | **5.03** | **0.08** | +43% |
| top-2 by 60-night score | +0.57% | 5.77 | 0.10 | +74% |
| top-2 by 120-night score | +0.67% | 6.51 | 0.10 | +87% |
| fixed NVDL | +0.46% | 2.71 | **0.17** | +78% |
| fixed equal-weight, all 14 names | +0.43% | 2.62 | 0.17 | +72% |
| fixed METU | +0.19% | 3.74 | 0.05 | +18% |

The ranked variants keep rotating into MUU and SOXL. Those have sd of 6–7% a night and fat earnings tails. That doubles the variance without improving risk-adjusted return. **The 30-night rank is the weakest rule tested.**

## 4. Robustness of the NVDA-2x edge
- **NVDL excluding NVDA-earnings nights:** +0.39%/night. Trimmed (5 best and 5 worst removed): +0.41%. Median +0.47%.
- **NVDL by month:** positive in 9 of 13 months.
- **Largest non-earnings NVDL nights:** −8.4%, +7.2%, −6.6%, −6.4%.
- **METU excluding earnings:** +0.02%, median −0.06%. Positive in 5 of 13 months. Its 30-night #1 rank comes entirely from September–October.
- **Spreads at 09:50 on 10/13:** NVDL 35.34/35.35 and NVDX 19.88/19.89. One cent each, so execution cost is unchanged.

## Decision → RULES_v3 §3 amendment v1.5
- The basket core becomes **NVDA-2x (NVDL + NVDX)**.
- A non-NVDA name joins only if it beats the core on the 250-night test (rules in §3).

**Expected effect:** about +0.40%/night on about 95% of E, versus +0.18% for the old basket at the same sd. That is roughly **+$0.40/night instead of +$0.18** at E ≈ $105.

**Risks accepted:**
- Single-underlying concentration (NVDA). This replaces the META concentration we already had (METU was about 64% of E).
- NVDA earnings night (11/17) → cash that night.
