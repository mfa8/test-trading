# NVDL hold: decision memo (Mon 9 Nov, ~08:30 ET)

## 1. What happened
Confirmed: on Sunday Nvidia's board opened an independent review of Jensen Huang. It set no timeline and has "confidence in Jensen" [1]. Huang admitted an affair and remains CEO. Trump backs him, and China calls the story "smears" [2]. House chairs have requested, not subpoenaed, a briefing from him on Nov 18 [3]. There is no 8-K filing yet [4]. At 08:21, NVDA was down 5.5% and QQQ up 0.7%, so the move is Nvidia-only.
Reported only, from anonymous sources: a US memo says the woman was recruited by Chinese intelligence [2][5].

## 2. Extra risk before earnings
Six held nights remain. The 11/17 earnings night is already in cash [6].
- Friday's options, priced before the news, implied about ±2-2.4% per NVDA night. That is ±4-5% a night for NVDL and ±11-13% over six nights. Live volatility is probably higher.
- Chance over six nights of an NVDA night of -5% or worse: 10-25%. Of -9.8% or worse: 1-5%.
- History: after 19 Nvidia-only gaps since 1999, the next night averaged -0.25% to -1.06%, against +0.12% normally. Not statistically reliable. Swings ran 1.8x normal; no crash runs followed after 2005.
- In 15 CEO-scandal cases [7], slow bleed was the commonest harm: a 2x overnight loss of 10%+ within 10 nights hit 6, against a normal 8-15%. Board reviews take weeks to months [8].
- After this morning's exit, the PAUSE cushion is roughly a 9-10% cumulative NVDL fall. Approximate; recompute from the account.
- My rough simulation (UNVERIFIED) puts the chance of hitting PAUSE before earnings at 19-33%, or 40-48% under stress.

## 3. Options
(a) Keep the rules. Expected gain +1.1-1.6% of equity if the planning edge holds (past gaps like this showed none). PAUSE chance is 19-33%. PAUSE then means 1 share for 5 sessions.
(b) Hold cash until 11/18 15:52. Forgoes about $1 (1.1-1.6%) and removes this risk. But the House date and the Shenzhen APEC summit fall on 11/18-19 [9], and the review could take months.
(c) Trade smaller, in whole shares; one NVDL share is about 25% of equity. Two shares forgo 0.5-0.7% with a 5-11% PAUSE chance. One share forgoes 0.8-1.2% with under 1% PAUSE chance. Both are rule changes: PAUSE only acts at 80% of the 20-session high.

## 4. Recommendation
Keep (a). Evidence of extra harm is weak. §6 accepts single-stock gap risk, and PAUSE is the built-in brake. §3(g) forbids rule changes on drawdowns, so the agent cannot deviate on its own.
If a roughly 1-in-4 PAUSE risk is too much, choose (c) at 2 shares for these six nights, costing about 50 cents in expectation. Give an explicit instruction or a dated rule amendment before 15:48. If live 11/13 option volatility is above about 70% after 09:45, I lean to (c).
