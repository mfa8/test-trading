Overnight decomposition (NVDL, 25 nights to 9/16, 24h bars): post-market 16:00-20:00 +0.77%/night (win 76%, sd 2.0) | 24h session 20:00-04:00 +0.20% (win 68%) | pre-market 04:00-09:30 +0.23% (win 56%, sd 1.4) | total +1.21%. ~63% of the edge accrues in the 4 hours after the close. Full close->open hold stays the rule (all three segments positive); a post-market-only hold (sell 19:59 extended_hours) would keep most of the edge, BUT it is a same-day round trip (15:52 buy -> 19:59 sell) = a PDT day trade, so it is NOT usable as a routine de-risk mode (amended 2026-10-10 by the agent; §6 PAUSE sizing — 1 share of #1 — is the de-risk mechanism). NVDL/METU/NVDQ are all_day tradable; TZA/SOXS are not. ## §3 Ranking & sizing (Monday ANALYST; daily sanity check)
score = mean(trailing 30 overnight returns close->next open) / stdev; eligible if mean > 0 AND win% >= 50%.
Hold the top 2 eligible DISTINCT underlyings, 1 share each, if both affordable; else 2 shares of #1 if affordable; else 1 share of #1. Target 70-100% of E invested; never > 100% (no margin use). Sizing clause (v1.1, 2026-09-14): if the 2-name basket costs < 70% of E, add whole shares of #1 one at a time while total cost stays <= 0.98*E. Same-underlying products (e.g. NVDL/NVDX) count as one underlying; the higher-scoring one is used. Any leveraged-long ETF meeting the §2 filters may enter the ranking (the seed list is not a cap). Sizing clause v1.2 (2026-09-21): if a further whole share of #1 no longer fits under 0.98*E (prices ran up), a lower-priced eligible product on the SAME underlying as #1 (e.g. NVDX for NVDL) may be added as a partial unit, subject to the same 0.98*E cap; it is exposure to #1, not a third name. If nothing eligible: cash, keep waking, keep reporting.

## §5 Exit (EXIT wake 09:26)
Bridge to 09:31:05. Per position: INV-12 intent -> marketable LIMIT sell at bid - max(0.02, 0.15%) -> poll 6s -> unfilled 60s: re-price to bid - 0.3% -> unfilled 60s more: market order. A LATE exit wake (even 10:30+) still exits immediately — holding into the negative intraday drift is the risk, not staleness. No overnight stop orders (gaps make them useless; risk is controlled by §6). Timing evidence (2026-10-21, research/EXEC-TIMING-20261021.md): no pre-market (08:00-09:29), open-print, later (09:33-10:00) or gap-conditional exit beats 09:31:05 on 164 mornings once pre-market spreads (~6-15bp) are paid; the Sep-Oct first-minute fade (+24bp gross, t 2.1, n=31, almost all October) does not survive multiple-comparison correction.
