## §13 MARKET-TREND SIZING (engine v1.8, adopted 2026-11-09 by the agent on the human's instruction of 11/9: "inspect our plan and see how we can get better returns, then make the actual changes ... fully autonomous"; evidence and every test run, including the failures: research/REVIEW-20261109.md)
(a) RULE: at 15:50 ET on every entry day (12:50 ET on a half-day, two minutes before the 12:52 entry) run `tools/trend.py state QQQ=<last> NVDA=<last> SMH=<last> --log`. QQQ's last price P is compared with L = the mean of its previous 199 official closes (the same test as "price vs its 200-day simple average including today"). P >= L -> UP, f = 0.98. P < L -> DOWN, f = 0.25. No hysteresis and no confirmation days. The engine trades every night: DOWN is a size, not a stop. Sizing from f is §3(c). The closes cache (research/trend/closes.json) receives the prior session's official closes at each 07:45 wake (`trend.py add`). FAIL-SAFE: if the tool cannot compute a QQQ state (exit code 2: cache short or stale) and the cache cannot be repaired before the entry, size DOWN.
(b) PRECEDENCE: KILL > HUMAN_STOP > earnings blackout §3(d) > §3(f) cash > PAUSE (exactly 1 share, §3(e)) > §13 f. §13 never sizes above 0.98 and never overrides a smaller size.
(c) WHY: on 26 years of day bars (2000 -> 2026-11-06), a synthetic NVDA-2x close-to-open position costed at 9bp a night grew faster and fell less when it was cut to a quarter while QQQ was below its 200-day average. Mean log growth per night, always-full vs this rule: 2000-14 +0.030% vs +0.096%; 2015-26 +0.184% vs +0.238%; 2000-26 +0.102% vs +0.163%; worst drawdown -93% vs -59%. The same rule beat always-full for 9 of 11 large technology names in 2000-14, 11 of 11 in 2015-26 and 10 of 11 overall (AMD is the exception). MECHANISM, stated honestly: nights with QQQ below its average are not reliably losing nights; they are 1.5-2x more volatile (NVDA-2x overnight sd 4.6-6.5% vs 3.0-3.3%), and at 2x that variance eats the compound return (growth with QQQ below: +0.006% a night when NVDA itself was above its own average, -0.270% when both were below, against +0.327% with both above). NVDA's OWN average is NOT used: with QQQ above its average, NVDA below its own was a normal state (+0.256% a night, n 725), so the market trend is the driver. NVDA's and SMH's own states are logged as shadows only.
(d) HONEST LIMITS: this is one survivor of a search (8 states known at the close x 2 symbols, 4 average lengths, 5 rule variants, 4 defensive sizes), so its true benefit is smaller than the tested one. It is a drawdown and variance control, not a forecast. It costs money in V-shaped recoveries (it lost to always-full on the index ETFs in 2010-14, and would have in rebounds like 2020 and 2025); the expected cost when it is wrong is about 0.02-0.09 percentage points a night. Within about 1% of the line the state carries little information and can flip from day to day. On DOWN nights at E near $88 the position is 1 NVDL share (about a quarter of E), so expected gains are small too. The synthetic 2x series is not NVDL (no fees, financing or tracking error modelled beyond the flat 9bp). INDEPENDENT CHECK (2026-11-09, separate agent, own loader and code; research/REVIEW-20261109.md, research/lab/20261109/verify/): every number in (c) reproduced; no look-ahead (state lagged a day: +0.163 -> +0.165); positive in all 72 sensitivity cells (QQQ/SPY x 100-250 days x size 0/0.25/0.5 x 3 periods); a shifted-state placebo never reached the observed gain; rule minus always-full 2000-26 = +0.060% a night, year-block 90% interval +0.009 to +0.121. VERDICT "weakly supported": ten nights supply about 98% of the gain and 2008 + 2022 about 73%; the rule lost in 51 of 82 below-the-line spells, in 9 of 22 years with DOWN nights and in 2010-14; neither half is significant alone; on the real NVDL there are only 86 DOWN nights (+0.020, t 0.55). So §13 is INSURANCE against a 2008- or 2022-style stretch, paid for with small whipsaw losses in most years; it is not to be reported or relied on as an edge of 0.06% a night. Rollback (f)(i) was not triggered.
(e) SHADOWS: the nightly `--log` rows (QQQ, NVDA, SMH: price, flip level, distance, state) in research/trend/log.csv; on every DOWN night the DAY report and state.today record what f = 0.98 would have bought and, next morning, what it would have made (the full-size counterfactual). The own-trend variants (NVDA or SMH below their own average) stay shadows until a quarterly review.
(f) ROLLBACK (pre-registered): NOT on live P&L alone: 40 DOWN nights cannot separate +/-0.3% a night (standard error about 0.8%). Reviewed at each quarterly review (first 2027-01-11) by re-running the long-history test with the new data and auditing the shadow. Revert to always f = 0.98 if (i) an error is found in the evidence, including a failed independent re-derivation; (ii) the updated 2000 -> present test no longer shows this rule ahead of always-full for NVDA-2x AND for at least 8 of the 11 names; or (iii) the human says so.
(g) TRANSITION: in force from the night-45 entry (Tue 2026-11-10: state at 15:50, entry at 15:52). Night 44 (NVDL 3 + NVDX 1, entered 11/9 under v1.6 sizing) is NOT resized mid-hold and exits at 09:31:05 on 11/10 as usual: this is an entry-sizing rule; on 11/9 QQQ closed about 0.35% below the line, where the state says little; and an after-hours sale would be an untested discretionary round trip. The §3(g) freeze ("no rule changes on 20- or 60-night drawdowns") does not block this: the change follows an explicit human instruction and rests on 26 years of data, not on the last 43 nights.
(h) FIRST LIVE FRACTIONAL ORDER: call review_equity_order with the exact dollar-based order before the first live top-up and log its order_checks; any alert that requires acknowledgement -> do not send, keep the whole-share position only, and tell the human.
